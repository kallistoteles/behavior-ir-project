# Core redesign reassessment: what the prior evidence establishes

Date: 2026-11-01. Scope: read-only review of the retained local primary research and experiment records under `/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031`. This note adds no measurements, proof checking, production changes or amendments to earlier studies. Source line links below refer to that exact retained checkout. The objective is a different execution/evidence architecture with substantially lower latency; a constant-factor optimization of existing expanded records is a separate result. “Two minutes” is a hypothetical example, not an established production baseline.

## Finding

**The architectural problem remains open.** Prior work considered substantial redesigns, but the executable comparison did not implement the strongest delta-execution or sparse-checking forms. It explicitly deferred verified incremental compilation and sparse authenticated witnesses. Its measured maintained-view candidate recomputed complete J and co-checked Core; its independent checker replayed the full claim; its shared DAG still constructed complete Core output; and its Nova circuit required a full external Core identity/output bridge. Therefore those measurements neither select a scalable redesign nor establish that genuine incremental execution cannot achieve a materially different latency regime. [032 planning research, lines 5–13](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/032-transition-verification-study/research.md:5), [032 implementation inventory, lines 13–21](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/032-transition-verification-study/investigation.md:13), [032 dispositions, lines 85–97](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/032-transition-verification-study/investigation.md:85).

A concrete next candidate is **compile an admitted module into an ordered, error-aware incremental transition program; maintain exact semantic views under an initialization/update invariant; retain sufficient deltas and shared support for separately requested audit reconstruction under a new, explicitly versioned evidence contract.** This is new work relative to the executed prototypes, not a newly discovered idea: first-principles research already identified incremental compilation as an independent architecture, and 032 explicitly left it unimplemented. A sparse independent checker and accumulated prefix proof are separately assessable consumer layers, rather than mandatory work on every trusted local operation. This candidate is a source-grounded research hypothesis, not a selected or measured solution. [Derived architectures, lines 55–83](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/first-principles-20261028/derived-architectures.md:55), [032 explicit deferral](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/032-transition-verification-study/research.md:11), [reconstruction freedom, lines 49–61](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/first-principles-20261028/research.md:49).

## Nova: first use, reuse and proof scope

For C1/H2, three first-use producer/consumer pairs have contiguous median **68.818 s**. A separately frozen three-pair reuse group has contiguous median **22.487 s**. First-use parameter preparation is about **32.7 s per process**; reuse still loads, decodes and validates parameters at about **9.6 s per process**. Isolated verification medians are **1.226 s** first use and **1.243 s** reuse; the small consumer Core bridge is about **0.056 s**. These overlapping phase medians must not be summed into a new total. [Measured lifecycle table, lines 150–155](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/032-transition-verification-study/evidence/cost-model.md:150).

“Reuse” means checking exact consumer-derived parameter artifacts and verifying each new proof. It is neither a permanently resident verifier measurement nor reuse of a previous proof's acceptance. Therefore a persistent-process steady-state latency cannot be inferred from the 22.487 s result. A resident process might avoid some measured loading, but that is an unmeasured architectural hypothesis with its own lifecycle boundary. At C1, observed verification for H1/H2/H8 is 0.472/1.226/2.249 s despite constant proof bytes; these finite observations establish neither constant latency nor an asymptotic counterexample. Capacity cases begin at N0 and create one entity, then command-only events; C16 is allocated capacity, not populated N16. ## Trust, reuse and availability cannot be collapsed

A local engine can rely on checked ingress and preservation of J inside its trusted execution boundary. An independent consumer cannot accept producer-created checked tokens; it needs its own complete checker or a sound proof of the exact relation. A new consumer must establish genesis/anchor validity or explicitly trust a checkpoint. A checkpoint proves continuation from that point, not earlier events. [Consumer distinctions, lines 13–19](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/first-principles-20261028/derived-architectures.md:13), [composition contract, lines 58–78](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/greenfield-redesign-20261028/composition-findings.md:58).

Existing Core029 reuse is deliberately narrow: literal Boolean globals and exact typed count(select(T))≤K, classified local dependencies, checked complete initial state and exact Store/history/module/profile binding. Unknown/mixed globals use ordinary execution; removal/migration and incompatible/cold contexts do not inherit the same validity authority. Successful exact CAS establishes child reuse; conflict or uncertain acknowledgement does not. This already solves selected semantic rechecking, while records still repeat full parent/member/rule evidence. A redesign must change more than naming or reapplying that reuse mechanism. [Supported route and boundaries, lines 8–49](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/docs/incremental-validation.md:8), [remaining evidence work, lines 82–94](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/docs/incremental-validation.md:82).

Hash authentication establishes identity of supplied content, not complete state/query coverage, current authorization or availability. Operational input also includes removed/lifetime-used identities, exact lineage and module/profile/context. Equal live state does not establish equal future legality or command history. A data-availability promise must specify retained inputs, retrieval and reconstruction; proof validity can remain true while a requested historical export becomes unavailable. [Operational state and claims, lines 15–61](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/first-principles-20261028/research.md:15), [availability/publication, lines 80–86](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/greenfield-redesign-20261028/composition-findings.md:80).

032's Lean targets establish logical restricted results. The Rust/Core/model/R1CS/SHA/serializer/crypto correspondence and backend completeness are separately trusted; fresh Lean replay uses the same kernel implementation in a new process. Neither a valid proof nor a successful differential test discharges all implementation correspondence. [032 trust ledger, lines 27–41](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/032-transition-verification-study/evidence/correspondence.md:27), [032 mathematical limits, lines 25–31](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/032-transition-verification-study/investigation.md:25).

## What an authenticated query must prove

A sparse witness must establish both membership and complete coverage under the exact authoritative state:

- A lookup needs authenticated value or absence, with an accepted root and exact key/type.
- A query needs exact result equality, including omitted/extra members and range/absence boundaries. Checking every supplied row is insufficient.
- An empty query still depends on rows that might enter it later; invalidation cannot track only the old result members.
- A count, aggregate or reverse-reference view needs an initialization equality and complete maintenance for every affected delta. An authenticated stale count is still stale.
- A global obligation needs the complete applicable rule/subject partition; the producer cannot select convenient successful obligations.
- Ordered, partial Core expressions require checked overflow, lazy branch justification, first-error/rule ordering and command occurrence multiplicity. An unordered mathematical aggregate does not automatically preserve these observations.

These are explicit prior design requirements, not a completeness proof supplied by a Merkle root, RAM consistency argument or producer label. [Exact maintained meaning, lines 78–100](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/first-principles-20261028/research.md:78), [full checker obligations, lines 48–56](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/greenfield-redesign-20261028/composition-findings.md:48), [derived checker/compiler limits, lines 47–69](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/first-principles-20261028/derived-architectures.md:47).

## Candidate architecture and evidence needed to choose it

The candidate would make **the maintained semantic machine the execution representation**, rather than constructing a full historical snapshot/trace as the normal intermediate result:

1. At module admission/compilation, derive typed change programs and their exact retained-view meaning J. Preserve ordered and error-aware semantics; use exact reference execution for unsupported constructs.
2. At initialization, establish complete operational state and J, including lifetime IDs, references, applicable globals and query views.
3. On a request, compute the exact result and complete affected delta, updating J by justified change laws. Do not recompute complete J merely to use it. Bind context and exact parent; publish auxiliary state only under successful canonical publication.
4. Under a future versioned evidence profile, retain request/context, exact outcomes/commands, deltas and sufficient shared support to reconstruct promised observations on demand. Specify old-profile verification and historical exports explicitly.
5. For an independent consumer, compare a native checker from an accepted anchor with a sparse authenticated checker and accumulated proof of the same relation. Do not put Nova on the local critical path unless the selected guarantee requires it.

This is a proposed synthesis of previously identified mechanisms. The distinguishing implementation is the compiled, proved/validated delta program and non-eager evidence contract. It is not the conservative full-J/Core co-checker A, complete-history checker B or lossless JSON-DAG transport. The sources support considering it; they do not prove its performance or mandate combining all layers. [Derived compilation and comparison, lines 55–91](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/first-principles-20261028/derived-architectures.md:55), [first-principles consumer/architecture distinction, lines 134–160](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/first-principles-20261028/research.md:134), [explicit version/identity limits, lines 114–122](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/greenfield-redesign-20261028/research.md:114).

The hoped-for local work is affected computation plus new data, rather than all live state or old trace bytes. This remains conditional: a tiny mutation can invalidate a global query or many rows, and full requested export still costs its output size. The complete language remains the target; declaring difficult cases outside a tiny benchmark does not establish the intended architecture. [Cost variables and conditional locality, lines 22–35, 126–149](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/greenfield-redesign-20261028/research.md:22).

The unresolved gates are substantive:

- A Core-to-incremental-program correspondence theorem covering exact decisions, errors, effects, commands and promised observations; initialization and every primitive change law.
- Complete dependency/negative-fact coverage and worst-case fanout, not merely local positivity/count.
- Reconstructible, correct view state across cold start, module/profile change, migration, conflicts and lost acknowledgements.
- A versioned event/evidence identity and reconstruction contract. Keeping old expanded record hashes still requires processing their exact bytes; adding a separate root without a checked bridge does not remove that work.
- Independent sparse-checker soundness and exact original-event/history binding. If proof accumulation is required, prove/check the identity/observation bridge inside the relation or via a genuinely cheap justified mechanism.
- Actual absolute latency by consumer: warm local evaluate+commit, cold/rebuild, first independent check, resident and fresh proof consumers, full export; separate persistence, witness generation, proof lag and sustainable throughput.
- Measurements showing which N-, H- and affected-dependency work was removed. No retained result demonstrates millisecond end-to-end latency for this architecture.

These gates follow directly from [032 adoption limitations, lines 119–126](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/032-transition-verification-study/investigation.md:119), [greenfield discriminators and remaining unknowns, lines 165–181](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/greenfield-redesign-20261028/research.md:165), and [first-principles discriminating cases, lines 85–91](/tmp/behavior-ir-core-final-20261031/specs/031-scalable-execution-evidence/profiling/first-principles-20261028/derived-architectures.md:85).

The defensible decision is to reopen architecture selection around these unimplemented mechanisms and exact consumer guarantees. Finishing the existing-format optimization study does not complete that objective; neither does relabeling the old hybrid as a discovery. A millisecond target can guide future acceptance criteria after specifying workload and operational boundary, but it is not a result established by these sources.
